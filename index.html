English (short guide)

BON MUSEUM is a small fan-made museum of "Bon-chan", a cast of seven little spirits (Oko, Niko, Hyoe, Mogu, Nemu, Tere, and Niji). You can play browser games, chat with the Bons (script-based, in Japanese), take a personality quiz, and save wallpapers.

LINE stickers: available on LINE STORE.

  • Oko-Bon's "Punpun" stickers
  • Hyoe-Bon's "Hyoe Hyoe" stickers
  • Bon-chan Everyday Stickers (vol. 1)
  • Colorful Bon-chans (vol. 2)

Most pages are in Japanese. Thanks for visiting!
